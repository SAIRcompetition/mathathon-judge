### The result

Let $W^*(S)$ be the von Neumann algebra generated by a set $S$ of operators (with $1$, adjoints and weak closure). Kadison's generator problem (1967, Problem 14): is every von Neumann algebra with separable predual of the form $W^*(x)$, equivalently $W^*(a,b)$ with $a,b$ self-adjoint ($x=a+ib$)? For a II₁ factor with trace $\tau$ put $\|x\|_2=\tau(x^*x)^{1/2}$.

Theorem 1.1 (OpenAI): if $P\subset M$ are II₁ factors with $P'\cap M=\mathbb C1$ and $M$ has separable predual, then
$$\{u\in\mathcal U(M):\ W^*(P,u)=M\}$$
is a dense $G_\delta$ in the unitary group with the $\|\cdot\|_2$-topology. Theorem 1.2: every II₁ factor with separable predual is $W^*(x)$ for some $x\in M$. Section 6 deduces the general case and shows (Corollary 6.1) that for $n\ge3$ Voiculescu's free entropy dimensions $\delta,\delta_0$ differ on two generating tuples of $L(\mathbb F_n)$.

### How the known proof works

Structure as given in the preprint (Section 1):
- Section 2: $P^\omega$ has trivial relative commutant in the ultrapower $M^\omega$ (Lemma 2.1), so Popa's independence theorem gives Haar unitaries in $P^\omega$ free from any norm-separable C*-subalgebra (Lemma 2.3).
- Section 3 (Proposition 3.2): for $0<\alpha<\tfrac12$, a target unitary $z$ and $\xi\in M$, some $\tilde u$ with $\|\tilde u-u\|_2\le\alpha/\sqrt{2\ell}+1/\ell$ and $\tilde t_k\in\mathcal U(P)$ make $\tau(\xi^*\tilde t_0\prod_{k\le\ell}\tilde u\tilde t_k)$ lie within $\tfrac{4\alpha^2}{1-2\alpha}\|\xi\|_2+\tfrac1\ell$ of $\tfrac\alpha2\tau(\xi^*z)$. Uniformity in $\ell$ comes from a free-group convolution operator bounded by the length-one Haagerup inequality (Lemma 3.1).
- Section 4: the maps $u\mapsto\|e_u\xi\|_2$ ($e_u$ the projection onto $L^2(W^*(P,u))$) are lower semicontinuous, so by Baire all are continuous on a dense $G_\delta$ (Lemma 4.2), where a proper $W^*(P,u)$ would contradict Proposition 3.2.
- Section 5: an irreducible hyperfinite subfactor (Popa) has two self-adjoint generators, so $M$ has four (Proposition 5.3); with $M\cong M_2(pMp)$, $\tau(p)=\tfrac12$, a $2\times2$ encoding reduces four to two (Lemma 5.4).

### Background

- Kadison's 1967 problem list is unpublished; Ge (2003) summarizes it. Known cases: type I (Pearcy 1962), hyperfinite (Suzuki–Saitô 1963), properly infinite (Wogen 1969). Willig (1974) reduced the problem to II₁ factors, Sherman (2012) to finitely generated ones.
- II₁ cases: tensor products (Behncke), Cartan subalgebras (Popa), property Γ (Ge–Popa), Shen's generator invariant with the amplification formula of Dykema–Sinclair–Smith–White, sequential commutation (Gao–Kunnawalkam Elayavalli–Patchell–Tan 2025).
- Free group factors such as $L(\mathbb F_3)$ were considered possible counterexamples via free entropy (Sherman). Hayes (2026) gave a W*-bundle of II₁ factors in which no finite family of continuous sections generates every fiber.
