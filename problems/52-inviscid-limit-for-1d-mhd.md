# 52 · Inviscid limit for 1D MHD

**Tracks:** Computation/Applied Math, Analysis · **Area:** Fluid dynamics, PDE, compressible fluids

## Description

For hyperbolic systems in 1-D, we know the inviscid limit holds for small data, for general systems, and recent work of Geng Chen has shown that the inviscid limit holds for the Navier-Stokes to Euler in the isentropic case. These two proofs are not obviously compatible. Can we understand the intersection of the two proofs to show the inviscid limit for LARGE data solutions to general systems including the 7x7 MHD system?

## Desired outcome

Can the Chen-Kang-Vasseur theory handle MHD?
