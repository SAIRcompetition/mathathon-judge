# 51 · Non-uniqueness or uniqueness of Glimm-Lax solutions

**Tracks:** Computation/Applied Math, Analysis · **Area:** Fluid dynamics, PDE, compressible fluids

## Description

In hyperbolic conservation laws, the uniqueness or non-uniqueness of Glimm–Lax solutions has been open since they were first constructed 50 years ago. Recent work proves uniqueness for all Sobolev data, and an unpolished draft proof shows uniqueness for all small $L^\infty$ solutions outside a meagre set. The goal is to understand and simplify this draft proof, and possibly extend it.

## Desired outcome

A human-readable version of the draft proof of uniqueness outside a meagre set.
