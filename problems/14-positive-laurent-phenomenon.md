# 14 · Positive Laurent phenomenon

**Tracks:** Algebra/Number Theory · **Area:** Representation theory, algebraic combinatorics, mirror symmetry

## Description

The notion of cluster algebras was motivated by understanding Lusztig's canonical basis (Item 1). This has led to spectacular mathematics, including Fomin and Zelevinsky's classification of cluster algebras by root systems (Item 2) and Gross–Hacking–Keel–Kontsevich's proof of positive structure constants for theta functions (Item 3) in cluster algebras. All three items come with quite sophisticated ideas and proofs, which many researchers in the area are happy to assume without knowing much about them. The goal is to explore these.

## Desired outcome

For the classification of cluster algebras: a more streamlined construction that explains why root systems enter and how the proof works.

For scattering diagrams: an understanding of the rather difficult “induction on rank” argument that reduces the general problem to the rank 2 problem.

## References

- Fomin-Zelevinsky: cluster algebras II, Inventiones Mathematicae (2003)
- Gross-Hacking-Keel-Kontsevich: canonical bases for cluster algebras, JAMS (2018)
