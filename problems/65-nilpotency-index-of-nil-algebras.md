# 65 · Nilpotency index of nil algebras

**Tracks:** Algebra/Number Theory · **Area:** Associative Algebra, PI-Algebras, Combinatorics on Words, Ring Theory

## Description

Let A be an associative algebra generated by l elements and satisfying the polynomial identity x^d = 0. Its nilpotency index is the smallest integer k such that every product of k elements of A is zero.

Prove that the nilpotency index is less than Psi(d,d,l), where

Psi(n,d,l) = 2^18 · l · (nd)^(3 log_3(nd) + 13) · d^2.

Investigate possible improvements of this bound using combinatorics of words, n-divisibility, and Shirshov's height theorem.

## Desired outcome

A rigorous derivation of the stated upper bound on the nilpotency index of finitely generated associative algebras satisfying x^d = 0. The primary research goal is to obtain sharper estimates, ideally improving the asymptotic dependence on d and the number of generators. Participants may develop new combinatorial arguments, refine existing techniques, or identify classes of algebras admitting substantially better bounds.
