# Mathathon: Old Problems, New Proofs

Mathathon is centered around one question: how can we use AI to augment human understanding of mathematics?

Consider the four color theorem, the ABC conjecture, or the Navier-Stokes problem. Many mathematicians find their proofs—or claimed proofs—difficult to understand or unsatisfying. We invite our participants to pick a problem with an unintuitive solution, develop an alternative proof or exposition, then present it to their peers. The event consists of two rounds:

- **Round 1:** Onsite, 40 hours. Teams will make preliminary progress and give live presentations before judges and other contestants.
- **Round 2:** Remote, 2 months. Teams will produce an online explainer and a GitHub repository. The explainer can take any form: a draft paper, a video, an interactive game, et cetera. The repository will store whatever was used to produce the explainer—LLM chat histories, code used to produce visualizations, and more.

By shifting the focus from open problems, we want to encourage Mathathon participants to explore everything else we do to make sense of mathematics: generalizations, new notations and techniques, connections to other problems. These innovations can be more exciting than solving individual problems.

## FAQ

### Who runs Mathathon?

Mathathon is a registered student organization at Caltech. Its decisions are made by its organizing committee, which consists of undergraduate and graduate students currently enrolled at Caltech. The committee does not receive monetary compensation from Mathathon or its sponsors.

**Organizers:** Alvan Arulandu, Andrea Li, Andrew Wu, Andy Cao, Ashley Zhang, Avni Garg, Avni Iyer, Brian Zhao, Caiman Moreno-Earle, Hari Sriram, Iris Zheng, Mayla Ward, Merrick Hua, Milo Moses, Sathvik Rodrothu, Victoria Chen, Vihaan Dheer.

**Faculty Advisors:** Sergei Gukov, Terence Tao, Tony Yue Yu.

The Foundation for Science and AI Research (SAIR) administers grants and travel reimbursements on behalf of Mathathon.

### Who can apply?

[Participant applications](https://mathathonchallenge.com/participate.html) are closed, including the waitlist. Those who have completed a PhD in mathematics or adjacent fields may still apply as a [judge](https://mathathonchallenge.com/judges.html) until Nov 10 at midnight PST.

### Can I participate in just Round 1?

Yes. But you’ll miss out on a lot of fun :(

### Will my travel be covered?

We are covering the cost of flights for all participants and judges. Participants can stay at the venue overnight and work with their teams for the duration of the event. They are expected to find their own lodging. We are also covering hotel accommodations for judges.

### What is your position on AI?

We treat AI as one of many tools available to mathematicians. Proprietary models are allowed, but we encourage the use of open-source tools. We are partnering with the Foundation for Science and AI Research (SAIR) to provide compute, host workshops, and offer prizes to teams that use open-source models. Our goal is to empower each participant to make their own decision about the tools used at Mathathon and in their own work.
