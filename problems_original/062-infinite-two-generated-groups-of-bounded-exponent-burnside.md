# Problem 62

## Problem ID

62

## Title/name of problem

Infinite Two-Generated Groups of Bounded Exponent (Burnside Problem)

## Short description of problem (100 words or less)

Prove that there exists an infinite group G generated by two elements and satisfying the identity x^n = 1 for some positive integer n. Determine as many values of n as possible for which such a group exists. In particular, investigate the free Burnside groups B(2,n), their infinitude, and the dependence of the answer on the exponent n.



## Submitted Area

Group Theory, Combinatorial Group Theory, Geometric Group Theory

## Proposed Tracks

Algebra/Number Theory

## Desired outcome

A rigorous proof of the existence of an infinite two-generated group of finite exponent, together with an explicit description of the construction. Participants should identify as many admissible exponents as possible, explain which cases are known, and, if feasible, improve existing bounds or develop alternative proofs.
